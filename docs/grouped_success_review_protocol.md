# G3 conditional-outcome review

Apply the existing disjoint denominator audit to checksum-verified G3 histories. For every recorded-attempt window, inspect source movements at its response action indexes for the opening runner. Retain the parser outcome, attribution eligibility, SB/CS movement-reason presence and whether those inspected paths contain an out. Export every row locally and compact season crosswalks plus target-definition exceptions.

Do not interpret movement credit as safe occupancy at closure. An out in an inspected path is evidence for review, not a full completed-play survival label. Runner substitutions remain attribution exclusions; matching against the opening runner must not erase them or silently assign a replacement's outcome. Flag eligible rows with neither/both credit types, multiple attempt records, SB credit together with an out, or disagreement between the coarse parser outcome and SB/CS credit. The latter crosswalk check was added after the first audit exposed caught-stealing credits in the coarse success bucket; retain both audit runs.

This is a label-definition gate, not a conditional-success model or a new combined threat score. Choose and freeze a separate estimand, attribution rule, historical success features and chronological estimation protocol only after reviewing the source cases. Preserve all prior outcomes and keep 2025 closed.
